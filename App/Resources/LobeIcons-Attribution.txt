31 of the 32 named model brands have bundled upstream artwork. No Thinking
Machines icon was found at this pinned revision: thinkingmachines.svg,
thinkingmachines-color.svg, and thinking-machines.svg returned HTTP 404.
AIModelThinkingMachines is not supplied; no substitute artwork was invented.

SVG artwork is copied without recolouring or redrawing. Existing filenames are
retained where possible. Claude uses its orange asterisk, not the Anthropic
lettermark; xAI uses the Grok glyph. Amazon uses Nova, InclusionAI uses Ling,
Databricks uses DBRX, and Xiaomi uses XiaomiMiMo. Nous Research uses the upstream
NOUS wordmark; the larger portrait SVG could not be retrieved in full through
the available page reader and was not copied in truncated form.

Colour variants are used where available. OpenAI, Grok, XiaomiMiMo, AI21, IBM,
Liquid, Reka, and the NOUS wordmark retain upstream monochrome artwork. Kimi's
colour SVG includes both a blue accent and currentColor paths. No fabricated
brand colours are applied. All supplied imagesets preserve vector data and omit
template-rendering-intent. Native asset compilation and light/dark appearance
still require validation in the app build.

Unreferenced legacy kimi.svg, mistral.svg, and qwen.svg are retained alongside
the colour variants. No files were deleted. No runtime logo fetch, SVG parser,
or new build dependency is needed.

Brand names and logos belong to their respective owners. Inclusion identifies
models and does not imply sponsorship or endorsement.

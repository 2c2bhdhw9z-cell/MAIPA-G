These are the upstream monochrome variants, not recreated paths or SF Symbols.
Each imageset uses universal vector data and template rendering. AIModelIcon
inherits the caller's foreground style so the marks remain visible in either
appearance. No remote logo fetch, runtime SVG parser, or build dependency is used.

Brand names and logos belong to their respective owners. Inclusion identifies
models and does not imply sponsorship or endorsement.
